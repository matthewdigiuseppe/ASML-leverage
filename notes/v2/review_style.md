# Style audit of the v2 draft (tag: style)

Writing-style auditor, against notes/v2/style_brief.md and the previous audit (scratchpad ref4.txt). This is not a substantive referee report. It measures the prose, inventories the tells, flags verbs that overclaim about the record or the scenario, gives local fixes with replacement text, and lists the cuts that would take the main text from about 28,250 raw words (wc -w on sections 01 to 10) to the editors' budget of about 19,000 to 20,000. The paper was not edited.

## 1. Method

A script (scratchpad/style/metrics.py) stripped comments, the environments proposition, lemma, corollary, remark, definition, table, figure, equation, align and enumerate, display math, and citations. It replaced inline math with one token, \citet with one token, and \cref with one token. Paragraphs under 40 words were dropped for the paragraph statistics. That leaves 980 sentences in 157 paragraphs. Line numbers below are file line numbers in paper/sections/. Every anchor quoted in the findings was checked with grep -F against the current files.

- **Uplift endings.** One: 06 l.67 closes on "Advice that Europe should rearm ... can prompt an offer for the chokepoint itself", which also attributes advice to no one.

## 4. Verbs that overclaim about the record or the scenario

1. 08 l.57 "The record therefore supports the jurisdictional channel". The introduction (01 l.17) says "is consistent with"; use that.
2. 05 l.84 "The record fits the first half of this prediction." The first half is that linkage appears when the ally has slack. The NSS and the joint statement show linkage; whether Europe had slack is the paper's own reading.
3. 08 l.61 "A shared-interest account fits the substance of Dutch alignment but not its timing". A holder that shares the threat view may still wait for US rules to avoid acting alone.
4. 08 l.67 "The reversal under Chinese pressure is also evidence that compelling a firm is costly". Two paragraphs earlier (08 l.65) the draft says "The reversal does not discriminate, since by then the rule behind the US demand had been suspended." 07 l.29 makes the same claim and leaves out the Busan step that preceded the Dutch suspension. Both should be conditional.
5. 10 l.11 "What the record shows so far fits the jurisdictional half of the argument."
6. 00_abstract "We verify each result by brute-force computation." Grid checks do not verify a general result. The proofs are in Appendix A.
7. The scenario. Resolution 5 confines Europe 2031 to the introduction and Appendix C. Section 7 evaluates it three times in the main text: l.72 (the 2029 Council vote arithmetic), l.114 ("is a game of this kind") and l.120 ("For a measure confined to US soil the claim is plausible", which is also an impersonal hedge). 04 l.159 engages the "only card" quotation, which is a fair use of the hook.
8. 01 l.11 "A holder with a negative premium therefore gets nothing for its chokepoint unless the dispute risks rupture". This is wrong as stated. By Proposition 7, hold-up can give a positive transfer at lambda = 0. The "therefore" also hangs the first bound on the slack principle.

## 8. Verdict

The surface of the prose is clean. There are no em dashes, no dead vocabulary, no copula avoidance and no ornamental negative parallelism, and the stance markers are now agent-attached by about seven to one. Burstiness (0.54) and paragraph CV (0.40) clear their flags, though the literature and the conclusion are flat. The previous round's terminology repairs held for "instrument", "exit premium" and the channel names in the corollary, with the exceptions listed in section 3.

Three problems remain. First, repetition is now the paper's main tell and its main excess: the same facts, quotations and credits recur in three to seven places, and the conclusion restates the Limitations subsection and the test designs. Removing the repeats accounts for about a third of the 8,000 words. Second, four sentences overclaim: the intro's "unless the dispute risks rupture" omits hold-up, the Nexperia reversal is read twice as evidence the draft elsewhere calls non-discriminating, the abstract says brute-force computation "verifies" results, and section 7 judges the scenario in the main text. Third, "leverage" slips back into a broader meaning in sec:coding. Each fix is local and has replacement text above. Two replacement sentences need the author's check: the Cooley and Calder contrast (fix 36) and the ahtk20252026_1903 citation for the shared-threat account (fix 20).
