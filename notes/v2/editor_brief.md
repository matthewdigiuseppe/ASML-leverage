2. **New Remark: slack under rupture risk** (label `rem:rupture`), placed in 05_slack.tex right after Proposition 2 and its first discussion paragraph. Statement (frequent-offer limit; verified by code/checks/v2_rupture_bite.py, 13,079 draws, 0 violations):
   "Suppose Europe's payoff from alignment is the bargaining outcome $P^*(Q)$ of \cref{prop:master} rather than the bidding payment $\pos{Q}$. Withdrawing a threat set $J$ then costs Europe $B_\psi(J)=\Delta^A(J)-[P^*(Q+d(J))-P^*(Q)]$, and $B_\psi(J)\le u(J)+\min\{d(J),\Lambda_\psi\}$ with $\Lambda_\psi\equiv P^*(Q)-Q\ge\Lambda$. The bound holds with equality when the outside option binds after the withdrawal ($Q+d(J)\ge\hat Q$), and $B_\psi=B$ when $\lambda=0$. Every bound in \cref{prop:slack}(c) holds with $\Lambda_\psi$ in place of $\Lambda$."
   Proof (for A_proofs): Europe's payoff before is $P^*(Q)$; after withdrawal the aligned payoff falls by $\Delta^A$ and the exit payoff by $\Delta^X=u$, so the premium becomes $Q+d$ and Europe's payoff is $-\Delta^A+P^*(Q+d)$. Since $P^*$ is nondecreasing, $P^*(Q+d)-P^*(Q)\ge 0$, so $B_\psi\le u+d$; since $P^*(Q+d)\ge Q+d$, $B_\psi\le u+d-(Q+d-P^*(Q))=u+\Lambda_\psi$. If $Q+d\ge\hat Q$, $P^*(Q+d)=Q+d$ and the second bound holds with equality. With $\lambda=0$, $P^*=\pos{\cdot}$ and $\Lambda_\psi=\pos{-Q}$, and $B_\psi=u+d-\pos{Q+d}+\pos{Q}=u+\min\{d,\pos{-Q}\}$ (as in Prop. 2(a)). The one-budget bound follows because Europe can always refuse every ultimatum and absorb the withdrawal of $J^c$. Example: $Q=-1$, $V=4$, $\beta=0.5$, $\lambda=1$, $r=0.25$: $\Lambda=1$, $\Lambda_\psi=2.2$, and withdrawing $d=3$ bites $1.8$.
   Reading for the text: whatever agenda power under rupture risk wins Europe above $\pos{Q}$, credible conditional threats can claw back, down to the exit premium. Washington's dependence raises both what Europe obtains in bargaining and the slack Washington can coerce. The agenda channel survives only to the extent that Washington lacks credible conditional threats large enough to push Europe to its outside option.
