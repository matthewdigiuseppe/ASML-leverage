## Which Leeds (2003)?

Cite both articles, each for its own half of the claim. `leeds2003alliance` (IO) supports "alliance commitments are usually honored": about 75 percent fulfilled, 1816-1944. `leeds2003do` (AJPS) supports "alliances deter", but only for defensive commitments to potential targets. A combined sentence could read: "Defensive alliance commitments deter challenges (Leeds 2003b) and are honored in most wars in which they are invoked (Leeds 2003a)." Note that `leeds2003alliance` also finds major powers more likely to renege.

## Blankenship check

The claim that allies in strategically valuable locations resist pressure holds as a finding about burden-sharing *levels* in `blankenship2021price`: lower burden sharing where the US values the location. The mechanism is less credible US abandonment threats. The 2023 book does not list location among its three factors. Its Iceland chapter (pp. 111-119) is the qualitative case for the location effect.

## Warnings and tensions for the paper

1. Gerzhoy (2015) and Lanoszka (2018) disagree about West Germany. Gerzhoy credits US abandonment threats. Lanoszka says alliance coercion was hard and that economic or technological dependence is the stronger lever. The paper's dual-dependence framing benefits from citing both.
2. Leeds (2003, IO) finds major powers are likelier treaty violators. This supports European doubts about the US, not US reliability.
3. Kroenig (2009) finds no support for economic motives in sensitive transfers. Gray (2012) shows commercial motives driving Bonn in 1975-77. The paper should say which reading it adopts for Europe.
4. Fuhrmann and Sechser (2014) concern deterrence of adversaries. Lanoszka (2018) concerns assurance of allies. Do not use the first to make claims about ally perceptions.
5. Use print years, not the e-book years Crossref shows: Calder 2007 (Crossref 2010), Cooley 2008 (Crossref 2011), Gowa 1994 (Crossref 1995/2020).
6. Snyder (1997) page numbers and the Snyder (1984) quotation come from a secondary source (Onea 2025). Check them against the originals.
7. For Gowa (1994) and Gavin (2004), I verified the chapter structure and publisher description, not the text. Specific episodes need page checks.
